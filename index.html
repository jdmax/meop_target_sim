³He Two-Cell Exchange

Warm MEOP pumping cell and cold target cell, filled to the operating pressure at temperature and then valved off. Set the tube geometry and the pumping and relaxation rates; the transfer model runs in Python in your browser, and the Formulas tab lays out its equations step by step.

Background: J. Maxwell & R. Milner, “A concept for polarized ³He targets for high luminosity scattering experiments in high magnetic field environments” (arXiv:2106.06606), and J.D. Maxwell, C.S. Epstein & R.G. Milner, “Diffusive transfer of polarized ³He gas through depolarizing magnetic gradients” (arXiv:1412.6167).

    The model, step by step

    Every step of the model in the order it runs, with where each equation applies and the numbers it gives for the current settings. The cooler steps (1–5) set the target temperature Tc; the rate equations (6–11) then use it. Equation numbers refer to arXiv:1412.6167.
