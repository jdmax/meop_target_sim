³He Two-Cell Exchange

Warm MEOP pumping cell and cold target cell, filled to the operating pressure at temperature and then valved off. Set the tube geometry and the pumping and relaxation rates; the transfer model runs in Python in your browser, and its source is listed at the bottom.

Background: J. Maxwell & R. Milner, “A concept for polarized ³He targets for high luminosity scattering experiments in high magnetic field environments” (arXiv:2106.06606), and J.D. Maxwell, C.S. Epstein & R.G. Milner, “Diffusive transfer of polarized ³He gas through depolarizing magnetic gradients” (arXiv:1412.6167).
